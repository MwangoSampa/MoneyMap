# Peer feedback notes – MoneyMap

Evidence of iterative development: what real testers said, and what we changed because of it.
Fill in one row per comment. Keep the tester's words as close as possible.

**How to run a session (10 min):**
1. Double-click `Sample Test Users.bat` and pick a test user (see `sample_test_users.md`), or choose New user.
2. Give tasks without helping: *"Add R80 you spent on food today"*, *"Which day last week did you overspend?"*,
   *"Put R50 aside"*, *"Find a tip about saving"*.
3. Watch where they hesitate. Write down exactly what they say.
